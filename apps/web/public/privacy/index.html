Location and navigation

With your permission, Waybi uses your location to show your position, calculate routes, follow a journey and provide navigation alerts. The native app can use background location and audio during active navigation. The web map needs to stay in the foreground; a browser may pause location and voice guidance when locked or backgrounded. You can change these permissions in device Settings. Searching an address or calculating a route sends the relevant query, origin, destination, waypoints and sometimes nearby coordinates to the services needed to answer it. Location is sensitive even when no account is used.

Accounts and saved information

An account is optional for basic map and navigation use. If you create one, we store your email, account identifier, password salt/hash for password accounts, preferences and the information you choose to save or sync. This can include Home and Work coordinates, recent destinations, saved places, route history, place notes/reviews and companion journals or travel totals. Connected Google or Apple sign-in uses provider identifiers; Waybi does not receive your Google or Apple password. Apple sign-in is available only when its configuration is enabled.

On-device storage and cloud sync

The app stores settings and navigation state on your device, and uses secure storage for account sessions. Live GPS updates support navigation on the device; they are not continuously uploaded as a live tracking stream. This does not mean that no location leaves the device: route requests, saved destinations, completed journeys, reports and account backups can contain location. Route Watch checks a saved route corridor on the server rather than following your live position.

Service providers and international processing

Cloudflare hosts the website/API, account database and caches. Google Cloud hosts Waybi’s private New Zealand Valhalla route service. Google Places UI Kit supplies Google address search when available. Depending on the selected feature and region, requests can reach independent map, search, routing, public transport or official road-data providers, including OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap-based services. Waybi Map uses independent basemap and routing services. The web map does not load the Google Maps SDK; it uses Waybi-hosted NZ map tiles, OpenFreeMap and independent search/routing. These services can receive IP addresses, request details and technical logs and may process data outside New Zealand. Their own privacy policies apply to their services.

Usage, logs, reports and support

Waybi records API usage and quota counters to control reliability and costs. Hosting and upstream providers can process IP addresses, timestamps, errors and request metadata. Community road reports include a location, type and time so nearby users can see the event; do not include private information in reports. Navigation feedback can include route details or coordinates. If you contact support, we receive the message, your contact information and any attachments you choose to send. Support mail is forwarded to the operator’s support inbox.

Payments

Where subscriptions are enabled, Apple handles iOS payments. Waybi stores transaction identifiers, product and membership status to verify and restore access. Waybi does not receive your full card details from Apple. A future web purchase channel may use Stripe and its hosted payment pages; enabling a billing feature requires its separate configuration. Deleting a Waybi account does not itself cancel an App Store subscription. Manage subscriptions in your Apple account.

Retention and deletion

Saved account information remains while needed to provide your account and chosen features, until you change or delete it. The current service limits recent destinations and route history rather than keeping unlimited journey lists. Sessions expire or are revoked when you sign out or delete the account. In Me, signed-in users can choose Delete account and confirm their email: this removes the active account and associated cloud records and clears account-scoped local app data handled by that flow. Provider logs, support correspondence, billing-provider records and recovery copies can have separate retention; active-account deletion does not erase every provider log or backup immediately. Ask support about information you cannot remove in the app.

Your choices and contact

You can use basic navigation without creating an account, adjust location/audio/notification permissions, change saved information, sign out, or delete your account. Contact support@waybi.co to request access or correction, ask about retention, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify that a request concerns your own information. Avoid sending passwords, Apple/Google codes or full journey histories by email. We update this policy when the service changes and show the update date here.
