Tell us what happened

Include your Waybi version/build, phone model, iOS or Android version, app language, the feature you were using, the expected result and what happened. A screenshot and the approximate time can help. For a route problem, include the affected road or junction and direction of travel. Share only the minimum location detail needed; you can redact Home, Work, names and unrelated map areas. Do not email passwords, verification codes or payment-card information.

Location, voice and weak connections

Check location permission and Precise Location in device Settings, volume and the selected audio output. Active navigation may need background location to continue while locked. A weak connection can delay a new search or reroute. If guidance conflicts with signs or conditions, follow the actual road rules and stop safely before changing the app. Report an incorrect instruction after the journey or ask a passenger to record it.

Accounts, privacy and deletion

Account settings are in Me. You can sign out or use Delete account with the requested confirmation. Deletion removes your active Waybi account and associated synced information; app deletion alone does not delete cloud account data. If you cannot access your account, email support for assistance. Review the Privacy policy for provider processing, retention and your choices.

Subscriptions and refunds

If Apple subscriptions are enabled, use Restore purchases with the original Waybi and Apple accounts. Manage or cancel an App Store subscription through Apple account subscription settings. A Waybi account deletion does not automatically cancel Apple renewal. Apple handles its purchase/refund process. Do not send a card number to support; a transaction reference and the product name are enough to start investigating.

TestFlight feedback

For a beta issue, include the build number and reproducible steps. You can also use TestFlight feedback. Never operate the phone or inspect logs while driving; have a passenger record the issue or stop in a safe place. Features and coverage can vary by build and region. A beta availability notice is not a promise of an App Store launch date.
