A little world.
Along for the journey.

Meet Waybi the kiwi bird, Clover the cat and Sett the corgi. A room to return to, small adventures to send them on, and keepsakes to remember.

✦Three little companions
✦20 minutes to 12 hours
✦Postcards & keepsakes
01

They have their own little day.

Waybi, Clover and Sett move around their room and react to a visit. Tap a companion to interact, or pack an optional item for a trip. Their trips take a random amount of time, from 20 minutes up to 12 hours, and continue while the app is away.

02

Something small to bring back.

Returning trips bring postcards and illustrated keepsakes — letters, maps, snacks and other finds. Open an item to see its artwork and story, then keep it in the journal. Country-themed collections give different journeys a local flavour.

03

Your real journeys leave a memory too.

A confirmed arrival after navigation can add a journal memory and a country-themed keepsake. The collection is inspired by the destination’s country; an individual landmark souvenir is not guaranteed. Find the companion room under Me → Waybi & Friends, including when using Waybi as a guest.

Good to know

Do I have to play to use the map?

The companion room is there whenever you want a visit. You can use maps and navigation on their own, and return to your friends in your own time.
