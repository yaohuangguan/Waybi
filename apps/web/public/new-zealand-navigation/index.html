01

A map that moves with you.

Open the map with north at the top. Start navigation and the camera follows your direction, turning with you. Faster roads get a wider view; slower approaches and nearby turns bring the junction closer.

02

Choose your journey before you go.

Search addresses and places, compare available alternatives, and check published closures along your route. Parking discovery helps with the last part of the journey. Available travel modes and traffic information depend on the location and route provider.

03

Built around NZ. Ready to travel.

Waybi Map uses our hosted New Zealand basemap and local address sources. Overseas maps, search and navigation continue through global providers. Official road-event coverage currently includes NZTA in New Zealand and Transport for NSW in New South Wales; fixed safety-camera coverage is New Zealand only.

Good to know

Will a reported closure change my route?

Waybi flags a closure that matches your route and prefers an available alternative without a matched closure. It cannot guarantee a detour when the route provider has not incorporated that closure. Check the event details and follow temporary road controls.
