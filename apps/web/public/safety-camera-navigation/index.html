01

See what is happening on the map.

The Road events layer starts enabled. Closures, works and incidents have distinct markers; published line geometry shows an affected road segment where available. Tap an event for its source, timing and any published diversion. A point-only report stays a point.

02

Check before you leave. Stay aware as you drive.

Discover shows published events and Waybi driver reports within 30 km. Route previews flag matched closures along the whole route. During navigation, relevant events ahead appear in the driving deck. Updates show their source and freshness; unavailable data is never presented as an all-clear.

03

A camera reminder when it matters.

NZ fixed safety cameras are matched to your route. A brief camera strip appears beneath the turn card when a camera announcement is delivered; the driving deck keeps the details available. The source is published NZTA fixed-camera data, rather than a promise to detect every enforcement activity.

Good to know

Where are official updates available?

New Zealand uses NZTA road events. Australian official coverage begins with New South Wales via Transport for NSW. Waybi driver reports can appear worldwide. A missing event does not prove that a road is open.
