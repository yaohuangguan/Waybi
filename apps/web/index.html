WAYBI · MAPS & NAVIGATION FOR NEW ZEALAND

New Zealand navigation, with a little more local knowledge.

Waybi is a navigation app designed around driving in Aotearoa New Zealand. Plan routes, compare alternatives, see traffic-aware road context when available, discover parking near your destination, and get route-aware reminders for published NZTA fixed safety cameras — in English or Chinese.

Navigation built around New Zealand roads

Waybi combines clear turn guidance, lane information when the route provider supplies it, multiple route choices, traffic context, walking and transit options, and a compact driving interface designed to keep the road visible.

New Zealand navigation guide →

NZTA fixed safety-camera awareness

Published fixed safety-camera locations from NZ Transport Agency Waka Kotahi are matched to your route so Waybi can give useful advance reminders. The data is checked regularly and remains a driving aid, never a replacement for road signs or current conditions.

How safety-camera reminders work →

Route choices, traffic and parking context

Compare sensible route alternatives instead of forcing a different route just for variety. Where live traffic data is available, Waybi uses it as road context. For supported destinations, parking discovery helps you plan the final part of the trip before you set off.

Explore route planning →

Route Watch for the journeys you repeat

Waybi Plus can keep an eye on saved commutes such as Home ↔ Work and surface meaningful travel-time changes. Route geometry is refreshed instead of being treated as permanent, so monitoring stays tied to the route you actually use.

Learn about Route Watch →

English and Chinese navigation

Waybi supports an English or Chinese interface and guidance. Search remains location-aware rather than assuming a hard-coded city, while New Zealand-specific road intelligence layers add local context on top.

GOOD TO KNOW

About Waybi navigation

Is Waybi a Google Maps replacement?

Waybi supports Google Maps as well as its own independent map and routing layers. The product is being built so map providers can be changed without changing the overall Waybi navigation experience.

Does Waybi show every speed camera in New Zealand?

No. Waybi uses published fixed safety-camera information from NZ Transport Agency Waka Kotahi. Coverage does not represent every road hazard, mobile enforcement activity, camera direction or lane. Always follow road signs, current conditions and New Zealand law.

Can I use Waybi without an account?

Yes. Core route exploration can be used as a guest. An account adds saved places, history and account-linked features such as Route Watch where available.
