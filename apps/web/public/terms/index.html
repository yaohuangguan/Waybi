Navigation and road conditions

Waybi provides supplementary route guidance and road awareness. Maps, suggested routes, ETAs, closures, lane guidance, cameras and parking information can be incomplete, delayed or incorrect. A suggested route does not confirm a legal turn, available parking, road access or that a road is safe. Follow road signs, actual conditions and applicable traffic laws. Do not interact with the app while driving; plan before setting off or stop safely. Waybi is not an emergency service.

Coverage and availability

New Zealand is Waybi’s primary tested navigation region. Features, data freshness and provider coverage differ by region. Live congestion and road-closure coverage are not complete; an ETA is an estimate. Search and routing services have quotas and may fall back to another available source or temporarily become unavailable. Waybi does not guarantee every camera, closure or road restriction will be detected.

Accounts and responsible use

Basic map/navigation features can be used without an account. Keep account access secure and provide only information you are entitled to use. Do not bypass request limits, interfere with the service, impersonate others or post unlawful, misleading or abusive reports. Public road reports should describe the road event and avoid private information about people. You can request help or delete your account in Me.

Subscriptions when available

Apple account settings provide subscription management and cancellation; access and refunds follow the applicable store process and your consumer rights. Restore uses the original purchase and account. Deleting a Waybi account does not cancel an Apple subscription.
