01

Set it once. Check it before you go.

Save your commute in Trips and enable Route Watch with Waybi Plus. Waybi checks the saved corridor against official NZTA road events every 15 minutes. Open Trips to review relevant changes before choosing your journey.

02

A route to watch, rather than a day to track.

Background checks use the route shape you saved. They do not require a continuous live GPS stream from your phone. Saved route geometry expires after 29 days and can be refreshed with a new journey.

03

One part of your travel routine.

Route Watch looks after the repeat journey. Discover helps you try somewhere different. Navigation brings the road into focus when you set off, and Waybi & Friends keeps a little memory of confirmed arrivals.

Good to know

Do I need Plus to navigate?

Core navigation remains available without Waybi Plus. Route Watch is an optional Plus feature for saved New Zealand commutes. Its updates can be reviewed in Trips.
