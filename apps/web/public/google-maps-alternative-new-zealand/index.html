01

What “alternative” means here

A useful alternative does not have to pretend the established product is bad. Google Maps is mature, familiar and broad. Waybi takes a different product position: keep navigation focused, let the map provider be replaceable, and add road context that is useful for the journey you are actually making.

Waybi Map is the independent path. In New Zealand, it is built around a Waybi-hosted base-map stack with local address and road data layered into search and navigation. The product can still use Google Maps as another provider. That means “alternative” describes the product experience and architecture, not a rule that every trip must avoid Google infrastructure.

02

Why Waybi built an independent New Zealand map path

Navigation products can become tightly coupled to one vendor: one map, one search source, one routing path and one pricing model. Waybi is being designed so those concerns can be separated. The map you see, the place you search for and the route engine that returns a path do not all need to come from the same company.

For New Zealand, this matters because Waybi can invest specifically in local address quality, road events and region-specific datasets while keeping the interface consistent. Overseas, Waybi can continue using global providers where independent coverage is not yet strong enough. The goal is not ideological independence; it is graceful provider choice without breaking the journey.

03

The New Zealand difference is road awareness

Waybi adds public New Zealand road information on top of ordinary route planning. Published NZTA fixed safety-camera locations can be matched to the active route instead of displayed as generic nearby points. Official road events can appear on the map and inform route context. Route Watch can monitor a saved commute corridor against official events.

Those layers have explicit limits. Waybi does not claim to know every mobile enforcement location, every lane direction or every temporary condition. When the source does not provide a fact, the product should not invent one. Road signs, temporary traffic management and the conditions in front of the driver always take priority.

04

A calmer journey instead of a larger dashboard

Waybi keeps the map and the next decision central. During navigation, the important information is the next manoeuvre, route progress, arrival time and road context that is relevant now. Before the trip, Journey Brief can summarise the route, traffic context when available, parking near the destination and safety-camera matches.

The product also treats arrival as part of navigation. For supported destinations, parking can be explored before departure. In Auckland, Waybi can use Auckland Transport open parking data where available. Published capacity is kept distinct from live space availability so a static dataset is not presented as real-time occupancy.

05

There is also a companion layer

Waybi & Friends gives the product a personality that conventional map apps generally do not prioritise. Waybi the kiwi bird, Clover the cat and Sett the corgi live in a small companion room. They can take short background adventures and bring back postcards or keepsakes. Confirmed real journeys can also leave a journal memory and a country-themed keepsake.

That layer is optional. The map remains useful on its own. The point is that navigation can be both functional and a little warmer without turning the driving screen into a game.

06

When another map app may be the better choice

Waybi is still a younger product. If you depend on a specific global place database, a mature transit feature in a region Waybi has not optimised, or a workflow that only exists inside another ecosystem, that established app may be the better tool today.

Waybi is strongest when its product choices matter to you: New Zealand road context, bilingual guidance, provider flexibility, parking-aware trip planning, Route Watch and the Waybi & Friends companion experience. A fair alternative earns its place by being better for a particular journey, not by claiming to be universally better.

FAQ

Good to know

Does Waybi replace Google Maps?

Not in an all-or-nothing sense. Waybi has an independent map path and can also use Google Maps as a provider. The Waybi experience sits above the provider choice.

Can Waybi navigate outside New Zealand?

Yes. Destination search and navigation are designed to remain usable internationally, although data layers such as NZTA fixed safety-camera information are specific to New Zealand.

Is Waybi available without an account?

Core route exploration can be used as a guest. Account features are used for saved places, history and features such as Route Watch.
